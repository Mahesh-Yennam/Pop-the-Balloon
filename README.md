## ☁️ Game Overview

Look to the sky! Colorful balloons are rising from the ground and soaring upward into the clouds. Tap or click them to pop them into confetti showers before they float off the top of the screen.

Watch out for the **Spiky Bomb Balloon 💣** that floats among the colorful balloons—clicking it triggers a fiery blast and costs 1 of your 3 hearts!

---

## ✨ Features & Gameplay Mechanics

- **🌤️ Atmospheric Sky Arena**:
  - Gradient cloudscape with animated drifting puffy clouds and distant hot air balloon silhouettes.
  - Compact desktop arcade cabinet framing on PC, expanding to fullscreen on mobile devices.
- **🎈 Multi-Colored Glossy Balloons**:
  - Crisp vector SVG balloons with realistic 3D specular shine highlights and dangling strings.
  - 6 vibrant colors: Red, Blue, Yellow, Green, Purple, and Orange (+100 pts each).
- **🌟 Special Power-Up Balloons**:
  - **Golden Star Balloon 🌟** (+300 pts, golden sparkle burst).
  - **Ice Breeze Balloon ❄️** (Slows down all rising balloons for 3 seconds).
  - **Heart Recovery Balloon ❤️** (Very rare, restores 1 lost life).
- **💣 Spiky Bomb Balloon Hazard**:
  - Obsidian metallic balloon with sharp red danger spikes and warning indicator.
  - Striking it causes an explosion, screen flash, and deducts 1 Heart (❤️).
- **🔥 Combo Multiplier**:
  - Consecutive pops without letting balloons escape ramps up combos up to `3.0x Pop Frenzy`!
- **🔊 Procedural Web Audio Engine**:
  - Zero external MP3 files! Procedurally synthesized rubber pops, air rushes, golden chimes, freeze whooshes, bomb explosions, and victory fanfares.
- **💾 Local High Score Tracking**:
  - Best score, longest combo streak, and accuracy saved locally in `localStorage`.

## 🕹️ Controls & Rules

| Input | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Tap / Left Click** | Pop balloon |
| **Spacebar** | Pause / Resume game |
| **R** | Quick restart match |

- **❤️ 3 Hearts / Lives**: You start with 3 lives. Hitting a Spiky Bomb Balloon costs 1 life.
- **💨 Escaped Balloons**: Letting a regular balloon drift off the top resets your combo streak to 1x.
- **📈 Speed Progression**: As your score climbs, balloon ascent rates and wave densities accelerate.
